# Privacy Policy for FitMetrics

**Effective Date: August 2026**

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at FitMetrics ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use the FitMetrics mobile application, you trust us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we describe our privacy policy. We seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have in relation to it.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
FitMetrics primarily collects data that you provide directly to the app in order to perform health calculations (such as age, weight, height, gender, and activity levels). This data is processed locally on your device to calculate metrics like BMI, BMR, Body Fat Percentage, and Macro targets.

Currently, FitMetrics **does not collect, transmit, or store your personal health data on external servers**. All calculation history and personal inputs are saved locally on your device using local storage. 

## 2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?
We use the information we collect or receive:
* **To provide and maintain our Service:** To perform health calculations and show you your history.
* **To improve our Service:** We may use aggregated, non-personally identifiable data to understand how users interact with our app.

## 3. WILL YOUR INFORMATION BE SHARED WITH ANYONE?
We only share and disclose your information in the following situations:
* **Compliance with Laws:** We may disclose your information where we are legally required to do so.
* **Vital Interests and Legal Rights:** We may disclose your information where we believe it is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding potential violations of our policies, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person and illegal activities.

## 4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?
We keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Since data is stored locally on your device, it is kept until you delete the app or clear the app's data.

## 5. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?
We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 18 years of age. By using the App, you represent that you are at least 18 or that you are the parent or guardian of such a minor and consent to such minor dependent’s use of the App.

## 6. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?
In some regions (like the EEA, UK, and California), you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. Because FitMetrics currently stores your data locally on your device, you have full control to access, modify, or delete your data directly within the app settings or by uninstalling the app.

## 7. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy policy frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.

## 8. CONTACT US
If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us or contact the developer directly.
