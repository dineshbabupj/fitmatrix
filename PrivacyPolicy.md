# Privacy Policy for FitMetrics

**Effective Date: August 2026**

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at FitMetrics ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use the FitMetrics mobile application, you trust us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we describe our privacy policy. We seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have in relation to it.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Personal and Health Data (Stored Locally)
FitMetrics primarily collects data that you provide directly to the app in order to perform health calculations (such as age, weight, height, gender, and activity levels). This data is processed and stored **locally on your device** using secure local storage to calculate metrics like BMI, BMR, Body Fat Percentage, and Macro targets. 

### Account Information
If you choose to create an account or sign in using Third-Party Authentication (e.g., Google Sign-In), we collect your basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) to manage your account and synchronize your preferences.

### In-App Purchases and Subscription Data
If you choose to upgrade to FitMetrics Pro, your purchase history, subscription status, and an anonymous App User ID are securely processed and managed by our trusted third-party provider, RevenueCat, and the respective app stores (Google Play Store). We do not collect or store your credit card information.

## 2. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

FitMetrics may request access or permission to certain features on your mobile device, including:
* **Camera:** We request camera access exclusively for scanning food barcodes to log nutrition information. We **do not** take, store, or transmit photos or video recordings to our servers.

## 3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND APIS

To provide a seamless experience, we use the following third-party services:
* **Firebase & Google Sign-In:** Used for secure user authentication and account management.
* **RevenueCat:** Used for managing in-app purchases and Premium subscriptions.
* **OpenFoodFacts:** When you search for a food item or scan a barcode, the query (barcode number or text) is sent to OpenFoodFacts to retrieve nutritional data. No personal user data is sent with these requests.

These third-party services have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they handle such information.

## 4. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?
We use the information we collect or receive:
* **To provide and maintain our Service:** To perform health calculations, manage your Premium subscription, and log your food/water.
* **To manage your account:** To allow you to log in securely and maintain your preferences.
* **To improve our Service:** We may use aggregated, non-personally identifiable data to understand how users interact with our app and fix crashes.

## 5. WILL YOUR INFORMATION BE SHARED WITH ANYONE?
We only share and disclose your information in the following situations:
* **Service Providers:** We share necessary data with the third-party services mentioned above (e.g., Firebase, RevenueCat) solely to operate the App.
* **Compliance with Laws:** We may disclose your information where we are legally required to do so to comply with applicable law, governmental requests, a judicial proceeding, court order, or legal process.

## 6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?
We keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. 
* **Local Data:** Data stored locally on your device is kept until you delete the app or clear the app's data.
* **Account Data:** If you have an account, your data is retained until you request account deletion.

## 7. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?
We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 18 years of age. By using the App, you represent that you are at least 18 or that you are the parent or guardian of such a minor and consent to such minor dependent’s use of the App.

## 8. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?
You have full control over your data:
* **Local Data Management:** You can access, modify, or delete your health data directly within the app settings or by uninstalling the app.
* **Account Deletion:** If you have created an account, you can request account deletion at any time through the app settings, which will remove your profile and authentication data from our servers.

## 9. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy policy frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.

## 10. CONTACT US
If you have questions or comments about this policy, or if you would like to exercise your data rights, please contact the developer directly.
