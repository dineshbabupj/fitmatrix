Built for Serious Workouts

Designed for gym-goers who want fast, reliable logging without complex sign-ins or slow server loading.

Structured Workout Logger

Easily log sets, reps, and weights. Features free-text exercise entry plus fast quick-suggestion badges for popular movements.

Dynamic Auto-Saving Rest Timer

Customizable rest timer (30s to 180s) per exercise. The app remembers your preferred rest time for Squats vs. Bicep Curls automatically!

History & Smart Auto-Fill

Saves time during workouts by displaying your previous session's weight and reps, pre-filling input boxes so you can focus on lifting.

Comprehensive Health Calculators

Instant body metrics calculations with clear visual feedback.

BMI Calculator

Track your Body Mass Index with color-coded category scales.

BMR & TDEE

Calculate your Basal Metabolic Rate and daily calorie expenditure.

Body Fat %

Estimate body fat percentage using Navy body circumference methods.

Ideal Weight

Determine target healthy weight ranges based on medical formulas.

100% Private & Device-Local Data

FitMetrics does not require an account or transmit your personal health records to cloud servers. All your workout logs and body measurements remain strictly on your phone.
