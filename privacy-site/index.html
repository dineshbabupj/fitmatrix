FitMetrics Privacy Policy & Data Management

Privacy Policy

FitMetrics is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect the following information to provide you with fitness and health tracking features:

  • Health & Fitness Data (Local Storage): Data such as your weight, height, and daily logs (food, water, workouts, macro targets) are primarily processed and stored locally on your device.
  • Email Address & Profile: If you use Google Sign-in for secure authentication, your email and basic profile are used solely to manage your account.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: If you purchase FitMetrics Pro, your purchase history and subscription status are managed securely by RevenueCat and the respective app stores. We do not store credit card information.
  • Device Permissions (Camera): We request camera access exclusively for scanning food barcodes to log nutrition information. We do not take, store, or transmit photos or video recordings to our servers.
  • Third-Party APIs: When searching for a food item or scanning a barcode, anonymous queries are sent to the OpenFoodFacts API to retrieve nutritional data.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. To do so, follow these steps:

Method 1: Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the FitMetrics app and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Account section.
  3. Tap on Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your account and all associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted.

Method 2: Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request data deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: support@trichy360media.com
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the email address associated with your FitMetrics account (Google Sign-In email).

Note: Data deletion requests via email will be processed within 14 business days. All your logs, personal data, and account information will be permanently removed from our servers.
